Frequently asked questions

Helpful answers before you call

How can I apply for admission?

Start on the admissions page or contact the admissions office. We will guide you through enquiry, visit, documents, and assessment.

What are the office hours?

The main school office is open Monday to Saturday from 8:00am to 4:00pm.

Can I schedule a campus visit?

Yes. Guided tours are available by appointment at 9:30am and 2:00pm on school days.

How quickly will I receive a response?

Most enquiries receive a response within one school day.

Who should I contact for fee information?

Please contact the Accounts Department at accounts@bachpanschool.org or call (555) 014-2214.

Where is the school located?

Bachpan Public School is located at 123 Meadow Lane, near Bachpan Community Park.
