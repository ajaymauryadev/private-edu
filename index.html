Frequently Asked

Ask and Questions

What age groups does Bachpan welcome?

We welcome early learners through primary grades, with class placement based on age, readiness, and a short family discussion.

How do we schedule a school visit?

Families can contact admissions to choose a tour time, meet our team, and see classrooms, play areas, and learning spaces.

What makes your classrooms child friendly?

Our classrooms are bright, safe, and structured for play, reading, group work, hands-on activities, and calm focused learning.

How do teachers share student progress?

Teachers send regular updates and meet families throughout the year to discuss growth, confidence, and next learning goals.
