Admission requirements

Grade-level guidance for families

Use these accordion cards to review documents, age guidance, eligibility, and notes before applying.

Nursery

Documents: Birth certificate, passport photos, parent ID proof.

Age criteria: 3+ years by the start of session.

Eligibility: Basic readiness interaction with family.

Primary

Documents: Birth certificate, previous report card, transfer certificate if applicable.

Age criteria: Grade-appropriate age and readiness.

Eligibility: Academic records and student interaction.

Middle School

Documents: Reports, transfer certificate, ID proof, medical certificate.

Age criteria: Based on completed prior grade.

Eligibility: Entrance assessment and parent meeting.

High School

Documents: Previous transcripts, transfer certificate, ID proof, subject preference form.

Age criteria: Based on prior academic record.

Eligibility: Assessment, interview, and seat availability.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions from families

How do I apply?

Start by contacting admissions or submitting an enquiry. Our team will guide you through the form, documents, visit, and assessment.

What documents are required?

Most applicants need a birth certificate, photos, prior report card, ID proof, and transfer certificate if joining from another school.

Are scholarships available?

Yes. Academic, sports, arts, and merit scholarships are reviewed during the admissions process.

Can parents visit the campus?

Yes. Campus visits are available by appointment throughout the admissions season.

When does the academic session begin?

The 2026-27 academic session begins on 15 June 2026.
