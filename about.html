Welcome

Welcome to Our School

Founded on the belief that every child deserves to feel known, challenged, and encouraged, Bachpan Public School brings families and educators together around meaningful growth.

Our classrooms combine clear instruction with inquiry, discussion, teamwork, and hands-on discovery. Students learn core subjects while building the confidence and character they need beyond school.

We value close family partnerships, safe routines, and joyful school experiences that help students arrive each day ready to participate.

"A good school does more than teach lessons. It helps children discover who they can become."

Purpose and principles

Mission, vision, and values

Our Mission

To cultivate confident, compassionate learners who think clearly, act responsibly, and contribute positively to their communities.

Our Vision

To be a school where every child feels seen, supported, and inspired to pursue excellence with curiosity and courage.

Our Values

Respect, excellence, creativity, leadership, community, and wellbeing guide how we learn and live together each day.

Our story

Growing with our students and families

From a small neighborhood academy to a full K-12 learning community, Bachpan has grown by staying focused on care, quality teaching, and a strong sense of belonging.

1999

Bachpan opens

The school begins with a small group of classrooms and a commitment to personal attention.

2008

Campus expands

New learning spaces support science, arts, library programs, and outdoor play.

2017

Inquiry learning grows

Project-based learning and student leadership programs become part of everyday school life.

2026

A modern learning community

Bachpan continues to combine academic ambition with wellbeing, creativity, and community connection.
