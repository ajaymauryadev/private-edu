Reading week inspires lifelong curiosity

Students, families, and teachers came together for a joyful celebration of books, stories, and lifelong learning.

By Maya Chen Published 10 Jul 2026 Updated 12 Jul 2026 5 min read
420+ books shared during reading week

During our annual reading week, the Bachpan community transformed classrooms, corridors, and the library into spaces for imagination, conversation, and shared discovery.

Creating joyful reading habits

The week featured author visits, reading challenges, story circles, and quiet reading time in the library. Students were encouraged to explore genres beyond their usual favourites and to share recommendations with classmates.

Teachers also helped students connect reading with empathy, vocabulary growth, confidence, and self-expression. Younger students enjoyed read-aloud moments, while older students led book talks and peer recommendations.

Tip

Parent Tip

Set aside 15 minutes each evening for shared reading. Let children choose one book and parents choose another.

What made this year special

  • Morning story circles for younger students
  • Student-led book recommendations in every classroom
  • A community book swap that welcomed families
  • Library displays curated by students and teachers
"Reading gives students a path to empathy, knowledge, and confidence," said one of our literacy teachers.

Moments from reading week

How families supported the celebration

Families donated gently used books, joined morning reading sessions, and helped students create reading goals for the term ahead. These moments strengthened the connection between classroom learning and home routines.
